Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how Apna Bazaar handles personal information when customers visit our website or place an order.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect your name, phone number, delivery address, order details and information you choose to send us.

2. How We Use Information

We use customer information to process orders, confirm product availability, arrange local delivery, respond to questions and manage customer service.

3. Sharing Information

We may share relevant information with service providers when necessary to operate the website or fulfil an order. We do not sell personal information as a business practice.

4. Payments and Security

Customers should never share passwords, banking PINs or OTPs with us. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for order fulfilment, customer support, recordkeeping and applicable legal requirements.

6. Your Questions or Requests

You can contact us to ask about your personal information or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.
