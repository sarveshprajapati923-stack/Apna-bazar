Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Apna Bazaar aims to provide customers with correct products in good condition. Please check your items when they are delivered.

1. Damaged, Incorrect or Missing Items

If your order contains a damaged, defective, incorrect or missing item, contact us as soon as possible. Please share your order details and, where possible, photographs of the issue.

2. Return or Replacement Requests

Contact us before returning any item. We will review the issue, product type and applicable consumer rights to determine an appropriate resolution.

3. Refunds

If a refund is approved, we will explain the refund method and expected timeline to you. The process may depend on how the order was paid for.

4. Order Cancellation

Contact us promptly if you want to cancel an order. Cancellation may depend on whether the order has already been prepared or dispatched.
